All links were accessed on **4 November 2026** unless stated. "Primary" means legislation, a regulator or an official EU or UK government body. "Secondary" means law-firm, standards-body or trade commentary, used where the primary text could not be retrieved or to cross-check dates. Where a fact rests only on secondary sources, [UK and EU considerations](uk-eu-considerations.md) says so.
